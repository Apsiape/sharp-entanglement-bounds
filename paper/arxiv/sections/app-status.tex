\Cref{tab:status} lists every result of the paper with its status. \emph{Proved}: a complete proof is given here, using
only the cited results named in the row or the proof. \emph{Checked}: a finite computation or a verified implication from a cited
manuscript. \emph{Conditional}: the result rests on a named input not proved self-containedly. \emph{Companion}: the result is stated
here and proved in~\cite{DM-game}. The final column describes the proof or finite computational scope. The references in the location column identify the written proofs; \cref{app:audits} gives commands, arithmetic conventions and limitations for the computations. Finite examples do not substitute for the proofs.
