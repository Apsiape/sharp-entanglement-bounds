\section{Proof of the white-box protocol}\label{app:protocol}
%======================================================================

We prove \cref{thm:protocol} for class~(S); the changes for class~(C) are at the end.

\paragraph{The interface.} Each party holds the physical payload of every copy and may append known pure registers. For
each question $x$ there is a reusable unitary $V_x$, with its inverse, acting on the payload and a retained local Naimark
register, and known outcome projectors $Q_a$ define the dilated PVM $P_{xa}=V_x^*Q_aV_x$, which on initialized Naimark
registers reproduces the strategy's statistics. A controlled reflection $2P_{xa}-I$ is implemented as $V_x$, then a
controlled known phase $2Q_a-I$, then $V_x^*$; no controlled version of the unknown $V_x$ is needed. Naimark registers
are retained across word queries, never reset. The same state and the same unitaries are used on every copy.

\paragraph{Thresholds.} Let $F^{(1)},\dots,F^{(K)}$ be the menu of \cref{thm:effective} at threshold $b$, so that every
strategy of loss at most $\eps_{\mathrm{cert}}$ has an array with $D_B(F)=\langle\psi,d_F(P^B)\psi\rangle<b$. Let $K_*\ge1$
bound $\sum_w|c_w||w|$ for every $F_j$ and every $d_F$, and put $\eps_*=\min\{\eps_{\mathrm{cert}},(\mu_{\min}/2)(b/K_*)^2\}$.
Then every raw polynomial mirror, including those of the $d_F$, has error at most $b$ by~\eqref{eq:opposite-poly}, so
$|D_A(F)-D_B(F)|\le b$, where $D_A(F)$ is the expectation of $\theta(d_F)$. Since the pair defect $C=F_jF_k-s F_kF_j$ satisfies
$C^*=-sC$, it is normal and $\theta(C^*C)=C_A^*C_A$; so $\theta(d_F)$ is the positive sum of Alice's own square and pair
defects.

\paragraph{Alice-only selection.} Expand each $d_F$, after $P=\frac12(I+R)$, as a rational combination of words in the
reflections $R_{xa}$, splitting complex coefficients into real and imaginary parts with phases absorbed into the unitary
terms; let $\Lambda_D$ be a common bound on the $\ell^1$ norms of the coefficients. On a sacrificed copy Alice picks a term
with probability proportional to its coefficient, runs its controlled-unitary Hadamard test and records a sign whose
expectation is $D_A(F)/\Lambda_D$. With $N=\lceil2\Lambda_D^2b^{-2}\ln(2K/\zeta)\rceil$ copies per array, Hoeffding's inequality
and a union bound give, with probability at least $1-\zeta$, all $K$ estimates within $b$. Alice announces the index of a
smallest estimate. A Bob-good array has $D_A<2b$ and estimate below $3b$, so the selected array has $D_A\le4b$ and
$D_B\le5b$: square and pair residuals at most $3\sqrt b$ on both parties, raw mirrors at most $b$. Because the copies are
independent and the oracles memoryless, conditioning on the selection transcript leaves the retained copy's $AB$
marginal unchanged; its global purification may be steered by measurements on other copies, which is harmless because all
bounds below hold for every purification and the environment and transcript are kept as junk.

\paragraph{Coherent sign rounding.} For one $F$ with coefficient bound $\Lambda$, a known PREPARE and a term-by-term
SELECT give a unitary $B$ with top block $F/\Lambda$; its oracle cost is the sum $L_F$ of the term lengths. With
$V=|0\rangle\langle1|\otimes B+|1\rangle\langle0|\otimes B^*$, signal vector $s=|+\rangle\otimes|0\rangle$, $P=|s\rangle\langle s|\otimes I$
and $W=(2P-I)V$, the walk $W$ has, on the invariant plane of an eigenvector of $F$ with eigenvalue $\lambda$, eigenvalues
$e^{\pm i\vartheta}$ with $\cos\vartheta=\lambda/\Lambda$, so the cosine of either phase has the sign of $\lambda$ when
$\lambda\ne0$. Phase estimation with $Q\ge1024\Lambda$
points, classified by the sign of the cosine and repeated an odd number $J\ge4\log(4/b)$ of times with majority vote, has
classification error at most $b/4$ outside the window $|\lambda|<\frac12$; applying the majority sign as a phase and
uncomputing gives an exact reflection $T$ on payload and work registers with
$\|(T-\sgn(F)\otimes I)(\psi\otimes|0\rangle)\|\le\sqrt b+\frac83\|(I-F^2)\psi\|\le9\sqrt b$. The second term bounds the weight
of the excluded window. Each $T_j$ has its own work register, retained when dirty; $T_j$ is an exact reflection on the
whole extended space, and controlled $T$ needs only control of the known classifier phase.

\paragraph{Dirty work does not spoil the decoder.} The ideal reflections $Q_j=\sgn(F_j)$ satisfy, by product replacement with
opposite-party mirrors as in Step~4 of \cref{thm:A}, every Pauli pair and mirror bound with constant $20\sqrt b$. Replacing
each $Q_j$ by the implemented $T_j$ inside a product costs at most $9\sqrt b+40\sqrt b$ per factor, again by moving the
right factor to the opposite party; so the implemented reflections satisfy all pair and mirror bounds with
$\kappa=200\sqrt b$. Both parties then apply the signed regular isometry of \cref{lem:fixed-size} to the $T_j$, using
$m$ controlled $T$ calls. Its Bell-stabilizer errors are at most $(2C_r+1)^2\kappa^2/4$ and the singlet infidelity at most
$2r(2C_r+1)^2\kappa^2/4=20000\,r(2C_r+1)^2b<\gamma/1000$. Adding the selection failure $\zeta=\gamma/8$ keeps the infidelity
below $\gamma/4$, and finite-precision compilation of all known gates, with per-gate accuracy below $\gamma/[100(T_{\mathrm{all}}+1)]$
for the total count $T_{\mathrm{all}}$ of invocations, changes the output by less than $\gamma/10$ in trace distance. The only
cross-lab message is the menu index, of $\lceil\log K\rceil$ bits, from Alice to Bob.

\paragraph{Class~(C).} Use \cref{thm:effective} for native binary constraint games, with the thresholds there. For each
variable $j$ fix an incident context $i(j)$; Alice's controlled proxy reflection is $V_{i(j)}$, a known phase on the labels
whose $j$-th bit has the queried value, and $V_{i(j)}^*$. Illegal padded labels are relabelled virtually to a fixed legal
assignment by known phases on the outcome register. Distinct proxies need not commute, and nothing assumes they do; the
rest of the proof is unchanged, with $\mu_{\min}$ the least incidence weight.
