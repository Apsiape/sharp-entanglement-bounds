\section{The raw cross-copy route for the point window stops at \texorpdfstring{$\eps^{-1/5}$}{eps\^{}-1/5}}\label{app:window}
%======================================================================

The lower bound of \cref{thm:point-window} uses copies $S_i=a^{3i}sa^{-3i}$, $T_i=a^{3i+1}sa^{-3i-1}$, $0\le i<N$, of the three-cycle
pair, and bounds every raw cross-copy commutator, a far commutator of length $O(N)$, by $\eta_0\le CN^ue$ when the far-commutator
areas grow like $R^u$; balancing $N\kappa^2$ against $\kappa\le CN^ue$ gives the exponent $1/(2u+1)$. This appendix proves that no
such worst-pair estimate holds with $u<2$ on the original source models, even at the scale $N\asymp e^{-2/(2u+1)}$ at which the proof
uses it. So this route stops at $u=2$, that is at $\eps^{-1/5}$, whatever the true growth of the areas; reaching the quarter needs a
different estimate or a different extraction.

The models are the collective models of \cref{prop:collective} with one particular legal choice of the spanning trees. The curl
equations of that proof may be solved along any spanning tree of interior edges of each region in which the source is a leaf
attached by its bottom edge: the holonomy at every plaquette depends only on the fields, and the protected supports only on the
labels. We use the row-spine trees of the proof of \cref{prop:raw-pairs}, for the region $B=R_5$ and for $T$.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:window-angles}
In the collective model with row-spine trees, on every row $2\le j\le M-2$ the only $a$-links carrying a nonzero rotation are the one
at column $M-1$, with the factor $\exp(i\varphi_BQ_B)$, $|\varphi_B|\le2\pi j/M$, and the one at column $0$, with the factor
$\exp(i\varphi_TQ_T)$, $|\varphi_T|\le2\pi j/(M-1)$. For $3\le j\le M-2$ and every clock $c$ of row $j$, the block $W_c$ of $a^{4M}$ at $c$,
a unitary on $(\C^2)^{\otimes L}$, satisfies $\opn{W_c-I}\le24\pi j/M$ on the whole qubit space.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The angles are those computed in the proof of \cref{prop:raw-pairs} for the same trees: a tree edge that cuts off a subtree $S$ not
containing the source carries $|\varphi_R|=\pi|S|/|R|$, the subtree below the spine edge of row $j$ has at most $j(M-1)$ plaquettes of $B$
and at most $j(M-2)$ of $T$, and $|B|=M(M-1)/2$, $|T|\ge(M-1)(M-2)/2$; the other $a$-links of these rows are not tree edges, and neither
exact move lies on them. Here each region contributes one rotation $\exp(i\varphi Q_R)$ with a projection $Q_R$, so
$\opn{\exp(i\varphi Q_R)-I}\le|\varphi|$ on the whole space. The generator $a$ uses the base links of~\cite[\S6]{DM-H3} in the convention of
the proof of \cref{prop:raw-pairs}, through their qubit-permutation representation, so without the rotations the $M$-step circuit of
row $j$ from any column is the qubit permutation of a colour permutation conjugate to the $P$ of that proof, with $P^4=I$ for $j\ge3$.
The product $W_c$ of the $4M$ link factors traverses the row four times and differs from the unrotated product, which is $I$, in eight
factors; telescoping gives $\opn{W_c-I}\le4[2\pi j/M+2\pi j/(M-1)]\le24\pi j/M$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The raw cross-copy obstruction]\label{thm:raw-obstruction}
For every integer $M\ge1152\pi$ the collective model of \cref{prop:collective} with row-spine trees, on $\Phi_{D_M}$ with Alice's
transposes, has all $22$ relators exact except H4, $J=-I$, every signed mirror exact, precision $e_M\le8\pi/M^2$, directional rates
$4M+4+2\log M$, and
\[
\|([s,a^{4M+1}sa^{-4M-1}]-I)\Phi_{D_M}\|^2\ \ge\ \frac1{1536\pi}. \tag{O}
\]
If $3$ divides $M$ and $N=4M/3+1$, then $[s,a^{4M+1}sa^{-4M-1}]=[S_0,T_{N-1}]$. Consequently there is no constant $C$ such that
$\|([S_i,T_j]-I)\Psi\|\le CN^ue$ for all $i\ne j$, all $N$ and all original source tuples of precision $e$ with exact mirrors, if $u<2$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The statements other than (O) are \cref{prop:collective}, which holds for every legal choice of trees. In the collective model $s$ is the
qubit swap $\Pi((1\ 2))$ and $t=asa^{-1}$ acts at every clock as $\Pi((2\ 3))$: the base links carry $(1\ 2)$ to $(2\ 3)$ and the rotations
commute with $\Pi((2\ 3))$ (proof of \cref{prop:collective}). The power $a^{4M}$ returns every clock to itself, so $a^{4M+1}sa^{-4M-1}=a^{4M}ta^{-4M}$
acts at the clock $c$ as $W_ctW_c^*$. With $h=\opn{W_c-I}$, $\opn{W_ctW_c^*-t}\le2h$, and since $s$ is an involution the group commutators
satisfy $\opn{[s,W_ctW_c^*]-[s,t]}\le4h$. The commutator $[s,t]$ is the qubit permutation of a three-cycle of three tensor factors, of normalized
trace $\frac14$, so $\|[s,t]-I\|_2^2=2-2\cdot\frac14=\frac32$. On the rows $3\le j\le\lfloor M/(192\pi)\rfloor$, \cref{lem:window-angles} gives $h\le\frac18$, so the
normalized defect at each of their clocks is at least $\sqrt{3/2}-\frac12>\frac12$; for $M\ge1152\pi$ these rows number at least
$M/(384\pi)$ and carry a fraction at least $1/(384\pi)$ of the clocks. On the maximally entangled state the squared residual is the
average over clocks of the squared normalized defect, which proves (O). If $3\mid M$, then $3(N-1)+1=4M+1$, so the word is
$[S_0,T_{N-1}]$, with $N\le2M$; and the defect divided by $N^ue_M$ is at least $M^{2-u}/(8\pi2^u\sqrt{1536\pi})$, unbounded for $u<2$.
\end{proof}

The theorem concerns the raw worst-pair architecture, not the game demand: these models carry $\Theta(M)=\Theta(e_M^{-1/2})$ ebits, the
order of the quarter itself. In them the shadow of $[S_0,T_{N-1}]$ in the plane of $a$ and $b$ uses only $a$-edges and has zero winding
at every plaquette, so its signed area vanishes; a bound by signed area plus perimeter cannot give the exponent $\frac32$ either. Every
filling of $[s,a^{4M+1}sa^{-4M-1}]$ needs at least $M^2/(8\pi\sqrt{1536\pi})$ cells H4, since all other relators are exact and the operator
norm of the word is at least its normalized defect; \cite[Proposition~3.4]{DM-H3} gives a stronger quadratic lower bound for these words.

At $N=4M/3+1$ the undiluted models have $N^4e_M^2$ bounded, while the window proof uses $N\asymp e^{-2/(2u+1)}$, at which $N^4e^2\to0$ for
$u>\frac32$. Diluting the defect on a flag sector of weight decreasing with $M$ reaches that scale.

\begin{proposition}[The obstruction at the scale of the window proof]\label{prop:window-dilution}
Let $\frac32<u<2$. For sufficiently large $M$ divisible by $3$ put $p=M^{3-2u}$, choose $M'=\lceil p^{-1/4}M\rceil$, and let $\Psi=\sqrt p\,\Phi_{D_M}\oplus
\sqrt{1-p}\,\Phi_{D_{M'}}$, the two summands in orthogonal local flag sectors of both parties, with the collective model of \cref{thm:raw-obstruction}
at $M$ on the first and a collective model at $M'$ on the second, block diagonal, and Alice's transposes. Then $J=-I$, every relator other
than H4 is exact, every mirror is exact, every state commutator vanishes, and the precision is at most $\bar e=8\sqrt2\pi\,M^{-(2u+1)/2}$.
With $N=4M/3+1$, so that $N\asymp\bar e^{-2/(2u+1)}$,
\[
\frac{\|([S_0,T_{N-1}]-I)\Psi\|}{N^u\bar e}\ \ge\ \frac{M^{2-u}}{2^u\,8\sqrt2\pi\sqrt{1536\pi}}\ \longrightarrow\ \infty .
\]
The original state has entanglement $h(p)+p\log D_M+(1-p)\log D_{M'}=O(M')=O(\bar e^{-1/2})$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The operators are block diagonal for the flags, so all exact relations stay exact, the mirrors of Alice's transposes are exact on each
summand, and Bob's marginal is a direct sum of multiples of identities on the two blocks, with which block-diagonal operators commute. The
squared H4 residual on $\Psi$ is at most $p(8\pi/M^2)^2+(1-p)(8\pi/M'^2)^2\le2p(8\pi/M^2)^2$, because $M'^4\ge M^4/p$; this is $\bar e^2$ with
$p=M^{3-2u}$. The squared raw residual is at least $p/(1536\pi)$ by (O), from the first block. Dividing $\sqrt{p/(1536\pi)}$ by
$N^u\bar e\le(2M)^u\,8\sqrt2\pi\,M^{-(2u+1)/2}$ and using $\sqrt p=M^{(3-2u)/2}$ gives the displayed ratio. Finally $M\asymp\bar e^{-2/(2u+1)}$ and,
since $p^{-1/4}M\le M'\le p^{-1/4}M+1\le2p^{-1/4}M$, $M'\asymp p^{-1/4}M=M^{(2u+1)/4}\asymp\bar e^{-1/2}$; with $\log D_n=4n+4+2\log n$
(\cref{thm:raw-obstruction}) the entanglement is $O(M')$.
\end{proof}

So the worst-pair raw cross-copy route, whatever area bound feeds it, cannot give a lower exponent beyond $\frac15$, the value at $u=2$; a
quadratic area bound would reach exactly that value and no more. The diluted models do not refute the quarter: their entanglement is
of the quarter order. They also leave the same-state quartic table target of \cref{prop:table} open, since an averaged table defect sees
the bad flag sector only with its weight.
