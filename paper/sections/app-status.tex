\section{Status of the results}\label{app:status}
%======================================================================

\Cref{tab:status} lists every result of the paper with its status. \emph{Proved}: a complete proof is given here, using
only the cited results named in the row or the proof. \emph{Checked}: a finite computation or a verified implication from a cited
manuscript. \emph{Conditional}: the result rests on a named input not proved self-containedly. \emph{Companion}: the result is stated
here and proved in~\cite{DM-game}. The review column names the
independent blind review that covered the result (see the section \hyperref[sec:methods]{How this work was done}); ``tool''
means the earlier review of the inherited tool results, and ``read'' the later non-blind second read of the whole manuscript,
named only where that read supplied or checked the written proof; ``check'' means an independent proof check, of the floor and
window results found after the second blind audit, or of the coefficient results found during the third; ``computation'' means a
finite check by the scripts in \texttt{verify/}. The twelve audits of the whole manuscript are summarized in that section and
listed in \cref{app:audits}; a row names an audit only where it was the first blind reading of the result or of the part named. The tenth to twelfth
audits were not blind throughout (\cref{app:audits}).
