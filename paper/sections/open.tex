%======================================================================
\section{Open problems}\label{sec:open}
%======================================================================

We state the open targets precisely, with what has failed and what would count as progress. The first two are the central ones.

\begin{problem}[The quarter lower bound]\label{prob:quarter}
Prove $d^\to_{G_{\mathrm{pt}}}(\eps)\ge c\,\eps^{-1/4}$ eventually, for the exact fixed game of \cref{thm:point-window}, against all original
finite-dimensional mixed strategies. By \cref{thm:factor-three} the reverse direction would follow up to a fixed loss rescaling. A bound
only on $S(\rho_B)$, only on Schmidt rank of pure strategies, or only on the native classes of \cref{sec:point-native} is not this result.
\end{problem}

The window is $[\beta,\frac14]$ with $\beta=1/(2\log107+1)\approx0.0690$. The proof of its lower end balances $N\kappa^2$
against a raw cross-copy bound $\eta_0\le C(A+N)e$ from the linear filling charge (\cref{thm:linear-filling}), so far-commutator
areas of order $R^u$ give the exponent $1/(2u+1)$. The geometric gap is open: with $A^*$ as after the proof of
\cref{thm:point-window}, $R^2/16\le A^*(R)\le135R^{\log107}$ for every integer $R\ge23$, and $A^*(R)=O(R^{\log\rho})$ with
$\log\rho\approx6.7325$~\cite[Proposition~3.4, Theorem~3.2 and Remark~A.3]{DM-H3}. Quadratic area would give $\eps^{-1/5}$, and
cubic area $\eps^{-1/7}$. That is also the limit of this route: the worst-pair raw cross-copy estimate $\eta_0\le CN^ue$ is false
for every $u<2$ on the original source models, already at the scale $N\asymp e^{-2/(2u+1)}$ at which the proof uses it, with exact
mirrors and only H4 inexact (\cref{thm:raw-obstruction,prop:window-dilution}). So the raw cross-copy route stops at $u=2$, that is at
$\eps^{-1/5}$, whatever the true growth of the areas, and the quarter needs a different estimate or a different extraction.
\Cref{prop:raw-pairs} refutes the analogous uniform raw estimates for lamp commutators below the exponent $\frac32$. One different route
is a same-state quartic \emph{table} bound $\Delta_N\le CN^4e^2$, in the regime needed for \cref{prop:table} and with all actual mirrors
counted, which would suffice at $N=ce^{-1/2}$ through a different decoder; \cref{prop:collective-refutes} refutes every smaller power
and the raw quartic aggregate shortcut, and the diluted models of \cref{prop:window-dilution} do not refute the table target, since an
averaged table defect sees their bad sector only with its weight. Another original-state operational method would also qualify. An
estimate or counterexample that moves the window is what counts; another conditional native layer is not.

\begin{problem}[The logarithmic floor]\label{prob:floor}
For every fixed game $G$ in the classes of Theorem~A with value one and no finite-dimensional perfect strategy, are there $c_G>0$ and
$\eps_G>0$ with $d^\to_G(\eps)\ge c_G\log(1/\eps)$ for $0<\eps<\eps_G$? The constants may depend non-effectively on $G$. A counterexample
needs one fixed permitted game and a sequence $\eps_n\downarrow0$ with $d^\to_G(\eps_n)/\log(1/\eps_n)\to0$.
\end{problem}

Every nonattaining game in the permitted classes for which this paper establishes a demand law has at least logarithmic demand;
the $I_{3322}$ contrast of \cref{sec:i3322} lies outside the permitted format, so it does not refute the floor. The precise state is as follows; every assertion is proved in \cref{sec:floor,app:floor}.

\emph{A class with the floor.} Every game that recovers, with actual mirrors on the original state, a sign relation $WY=-YW$ and an
even dilation $XYX^*=Y^m$ has $d^\to_G(\eps)\ge\log(1/\eps)/(4\log m)-O(\log\log(1/\eps))$, with an exponential fixed-target
threshold (\cref{thm:floor-class}); the Boolean constraint game compiled from Abels' finitely presented solvable group is an
instance, with value one and no finite perfect strategy (\cref{cor:abels}).

\emph{A criterion for a counterexample.} A finite presentation with a marked central involution, no finite-dimensional
representation with $J=-I$, and operator-norm models with $\opn{U_J-I}>\eta_n\to0$ and $\log D_n=o(\log(1/\eta_n))$ gives, through
the universal compiler, a fixed game of class~(C) that violates the floor (\cref{prop:criterion}); the compression of
\cref{lem:compression} removes any concern about the trace fraction of the negative sector.

\emph{Excluded candidate classes.} The criterion fails for the following sources. Thin Baumslag--Solitar models, whose operator
accuracy is exponential in the dimension: identifying the marker with their commutator kills it (\cref{prop:bs-central}), and every
fixed extension that places the marker in the normal closure of the commutator while retaining copies of the models needs a constant
relator error (\cref{prop:copied-gadget,ex:thin-bs}). Locally Schatten-$p$-stable groups, whose separated models have
$\log D\ge p\log(1/\eta)-O(1)$ (\cref{thm:stable}), including the higher-rank Deligne extension of $\mathrm{Sp}(8,\Z)$ (\cref{ex:deligne}).
Sources containing the dyadic sign-and-dilation pattern, such as Abels' group, whose separated models have $D\ge c/\eta$
(\cref{prop:dyadic,cor:dyadic-models}).

\emph{Obstructions to the method of Theorem~A.} The sign holds exactly and the dilation to every accuracy in every type~II limit of
the proof (\cref{prop:typeII}), so their presence in the limit does not distinguish the class of \cref{thm:floor-class}, which needs them on the
original operators at polynomial cost. Recovering Pauli arrays by bounded polynomials in finitely many tested projections can need a
doubly exponential coefficient budget, even with a hyperfinite faithful tracial closure (\cref{prop:slow-recovery}). An exponential
single-copy extraction threshold suffices for the floor (\cref{prop:threshold}), but is not necessary on general grounds: states with
distillable entanglement of order $2^{r/2}$ can fail single-copy extraction of $r$ pairs at every fixed infidelity below about
$\frac14$ (\cref{prop:separation}).

\emph{What would reopen or settle the problem.} A quantitative original-state extraction or compression theorem that gives the rate
for every game of Theorem~A, for instance a threshold $\eps_0(G,r,\gamma)\ge\exp(-C_Gr)$ at one fixed good $\gamma$, would prove the
floor; it must produce its decoders from the tested operators with controlled cost, which the type~II limit alone does not do. A
counterexample must give one fixed game in the permitted classes, of value one and without a finite-dimensional perfect strategy, and
original-state upper strategies with sublogarithmic demand along a sequence of losses tending to zero. One sufficient construction is
\cref{prop:criterion}. A construction through that proposition must avoid the operator-norm obstructions of \cref{app:floor}, including
local Schatten stability, the dyadic pattern and copied Baumslag--Solitar models. Those obstructions alone do not rule out a different
construction using arbitrary mixed states.

\begin{problem}[A dense spectrum]\label{prob:dense}
(i) Determine the set $\mathcal A_{\log}$ of logarithmic coefficients of fixed games of \cref{sec:arithmetic}. (ii) Find fixed games with
power laws $d^\to_G(\eps)=\eps^{-\beta+o(1)}$ for a dense set of exponents $\beta$ in some interval, or with another new asymptotic shape,
with complete two-sided operational accounting. (iii) Find a finitely presented exact source with a genuinely new noninteger or
intermediate demand law, or a dense interval of laws, with both an original-state lower bound and a finite-rank exact upper construction.
\end{problem}

For (i), the dense set $\mathcal S$ of \cref{cor:coefficient-set} is realized, with every positive rational and transcendental numbers
such as $\frac12\log_62$, and every element of $\mathcal A_{\log}$ is left-c.e.\ relative to $0''$ (\cref{thm:arithmetic}); the two classes do
not match. We conjecture that every positive computable real lies in $\mathcal A_{\log}$. A proof in the manner of the characterization of
the entropies of multidimensional shifts of finite type by Hochman and Meyerovitch~\cite{HM10} would need a fixed finite source in which an
encoded computation controls the density of addressable labels in every negative representation, at the growing depths of
\cref{thm:rational-ED}, together with finite-dimensional upper models of the same rate; no such source is known. For (ii), finite sums of
logarithmic demands remain logarithmic, so $\mathcal S$ says nothing about power laws. The only exponents known for the games of this paper
are $\beta=1$, for the configuration-lamp game (\cref{thm:lamps-ED}), and $\beta=0$, for the logarithmic laws; the point window of
\cref{thm:point-window} does not identify an exponent. For
(iii), the subset sources give the positive integers and the configuration and replication sources exponential order. Dimension
exponents, logarithmic coefficients and exact-source laws are different quantities, and none of these results settles the others.
\Cref{prop:finite-support} closes only the finite-support architecture with full stabilizers.

\begin{problem}[An unrestricted linear compiler]\label{prob:linear}
Construct, for every finite presentation with a marked central involution, a finite linear system game with split algebraic maps
and retained-state precision sufficient for the profiles. \Cref{prop:no-linear} excludes word decoders, so a polynomial-corner or
another architecture is needed; \cref{thm:universal} solves the case of Boolean constraint games only.
\end{problem}

Supplying self-contained proofs of the inputs of \cref{thm:native,thm:native-enlarged}, and of the restricted compiler of
\cref{thm:rational-linear-k}, would remove their conditions; this is consolidation, not a new discovery. Linear games with every
coefficient of \cref{cor:coefficient-set} already exist by \cref{thm:rational-ED}, so that compiler matters only for its explicit
specifications.

\begin{problem}[Remaining analytic gaps in the rational family]\label{prob:analytic}
(i) Improve the lower remainder of \cref{thm:rational-ED} to $O(1)$. (ii) Close the $O(\log\log)$ gap for $H_\infty$ at $\alpha=1$ in
\cref{thm:renyi}. (iii) Settle the shared-dilation source with $4$ and $9$, presently between rank $\Omega(e^{-1/2})$ and $O(e^{-1})$;
for an exact spectral law invariant under multiplication by $4$ and $9$, joint ergodicity and positive entropy for one of these
maps imply Haar measure by the theorem of Rudolph and Johnson~\cite{Rud90,Joh92}, but that exact-invariance result supplies no
stability modulus for the approximately invariant laws of our source models.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}[Directional normalization beyond agreement]\label{prob:direction}
Determine the optimal loss and resource conversion between the two directions for games that are not agreement games, or improve
the rescaling of demand profiles under a larger, physically specified contract. \Cref{thm:factor-three-sharp} gives a mechanism
boundary with Bob fixed, not optimality for all profile comparisons, and \cref{prop:sync-repair} gives only a square-root loss. In a
search over small synchronous strategies with non-difunctional off-diagonal predicates no loss ratio above three was found (the
largest was $2.997$), which suggests that the square-root repair is loose.
\end{problem}

Beyond these, two questions inherited from the tool results remain. It remains open whether a state-weighted central-involution
profile can be asymptotically much smaller than its flat profile. They agree up to constant precision rescaling for the matching
families computed here; the point-source window does not settle this comparison in general. And it is open whether near-optimal
strategies for $\mathrm{CHSH}(n)$ contain $\Omega(\min(n,1/\eps))$ distillable ebits; for that game only the entanglement entropy and
the entanglement of formation are controlled~\cite[Theorem~E]{DM-game}.
